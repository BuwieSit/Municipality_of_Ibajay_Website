Follow these steps to request and submit your municipal documents:

  • Select a Department Tab

    Click on the department above (e.g. Civil Registry, Engineering, etc.).

  • Download the PDF template

    Each service has a downloadable form or requirement checklist.

  • Fill out the Form

    Use any PDF editor or print and fill it manually. Make sure all required fields and attachments are complete.

  • Prepare a Digital Copy

    Scan or take a clear photo of the completed form and required documents.

  • Upload Your Files

    Use the upload button on the page to submit your application.
