Health Meets Tourism in Aklan!

The Provincial Health Office successfully conducted a Coordination Meeting with the Department of Health (DOH) on the Health Tourism Program, with Aklan proudly selected as the pilot site for its implementation. The meeting was held at the Provincial Governor’s Office Conference Room and was attended by representatives from the DOH Central and Regional Offices, Provincial Tourism Office, Provincial Planning and Development Office (PPDO), and Ciriaco S. Tirol Hospital.
